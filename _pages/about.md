I’m a Computer Science graduate student at the University of Utah, pursuing a Graduate Certificate in Deep Learning. My work focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer vision, with research in reinforcement learning, probabilistic modeling, and autonomous systems. I enjoy translating theory into practice by developing deep learning models, reinforcement learning agents, and end-to-end ML pipelines using Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn.

I also serve as a teaching assistant for Data Mining, Computer Vision, and Object-Oriented Programming, helping students strengthen their programming and algorithmic skills. Outside of academics, I’m an active skydiver with over 200 jumps, a watch enthusiast, and a fan of soccer and snowboarding, reflecting my appreciation for precision, focus, and adventure.
